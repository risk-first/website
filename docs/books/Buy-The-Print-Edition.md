**Software development is risk management. This book shows you how.**

Most methodologies focus on process. But process alone doesn’t guarantee success—projects still miss deadlines, burn out teams, and fail to deliver value.
This book helps you:

- Identify and manage risks before they derail your project
- Make better decisions under uncertainty
- Understand why methodologies succeed—or fail
- Adapt confidently as systems, teams, and technology evolve
