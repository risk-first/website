## Interview with Rob Moffat

A recorded session with the [TameFlow](https://tameflow.com) community exploring Risk-First Software Development — the background, philosophy, and key insights from the framework, and how risk awareness complements flow-based thinking in software delivery.

<iframe width="100%" style={{aspectRatio: '16/9'}} src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/6Qn9SJEmJMo" title="Interview with Rob Moffat, author of Risk-First Software Development" frameBorder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowFullScreen></iframe>

### What's Covered

- **Risk-First as a lens** — understanding software practices through the risks they manage
- **TameFlow and constraints** — how flow-based thinking and risk awareness complement each other
- **From theory to practice** — applying the framework in real organisational contexts
- **Decision-making and trade-offs** — why every development choice is really a risk trade-off
